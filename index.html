Problem Statement

Telecom Churn Is a Silent Revenue Drain

High-value customers leave due to billing friction, poor support experience, and contract dissatisfaction. Teams often react too late because model workflows are not repeatable.

Why MLOps

Without MLOps: ad-hoc notebooks, weak lineage, and difficult handoffs. With MLOps: reproducible runs, transparent metrics, and scalable collaboration.

  • ✅ Deterministic pipelines
  • ✅ Versioned data/model artifacts
  • ✅ Faster iteration with confidence
Architecture

Pipeline Flow

data_ingestion → data_validation → data_preprocessing → feature_engineering → model_training → model_evaluation

Pipeline Breakdown

1) Ingestion

Generate realistic telecom behavior + churn labels.

2) Validation

Schema checks, null checks, binary target validation.

3) Preprocessing

Deduplication and stratified split for balanced evaluation.

4) Feature Engineering

Create behavioral features tied to churn causes.

5) Training

Train RandomForest and persist model artifact.

6) Evaluation

Metrics + feature-importance + confusion matrix.

Case Study Impact

From Prediction to Retention Strategy

Model insights reveal that churn concentrates around delayed payments and support load. This enables targeted interventions in billing and service operations.

Wow Moment

Feature importance makes business action obvious: the model directly maps churn risk to controllable operational levers.
